# DR0088: qualified native learning rollout

Date: 2026-10-06. Status: source integration verified; fresh native release and final
aggregate checks remain integration gates. Historical DR0066 remains REFUSE.

## Context

The separately preregistered product-compatible study completed 1,500 admitted lives
and passed the unchanged 5% threshold against BOTH actual incumbent and additive.
Independent review validated every receipt, frozen input, corrected exact row-sum
comparison and repaired 56-row prefix. The original failed instrument attempt and
DR0066's incomparable inherited corpus are retained without relabeling.

## Decision

Only native creation resolves an omitted policy to qtable, and only for a fresh
Keeper without valid primary/backup history using both built-in inputs. Explicit
seed or covenant options retain omitted incumbent; explicit qtable remains usable
without extending the scientific claim. Low-level constructor/helper defaults
remain incumbent. The parser preserves None so it cannot impose a resume policy.

Existing primary or backup policy remains authoritative through resume, recovery,
automatic succession, hatching/elite adoption and explicit New without override.
Native New confirmation shows the effective choice and permits incumbent/qtable
selection for that new life only. All new lives start cold; current-life trained
tables survive exact restart. Conflicting resume choices refuse before writes.

## Consequences and limits

The measured aggregate gain is +11.7106982904% versus incumbent and +12.0719905870%
versus additive. It includes 451 additional begs and 415 additional Keeper feeds.
The prior declared product stance accepts this tradeoff with voluntary feeding
and incumbent opt-out; no human tolerance finding is substituted. All 400
nonattentive lives die in every arm. One attentive follower life dies at 180 while
both comparators live at 200. This is aggregate qualification, not universal
survival, attachment, individual personality or inherited trained memory.

Source tests and actual Windows SDL launches qualify the boundary. Final build,
full sweep, artifact/preservation/diff checks and exact-published-SHA remote CI
remain separately recorded. No scientific cohort was rerun for this rollout, and
no rejected care or mutation mechanism is promoted by the learning result.
