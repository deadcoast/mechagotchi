Keep covenant behavior as species priors. Individual inherited morphology supplies functional capacity; neither trained learning nor a hidden history hash is inherited.

Absent model remains legacy. Persist active model identity per life and campaign, immutable founders/era inputs/reproduction and visible history. Reject unsupported versions and conflicting saved/requested identities. Recompute from actual repaired bodies; preserve all food, repair, death, policy and sampling semantics. No X6 operative mutation adoption.

Prior refusals, saves, artifacts and builds stay preserved. The report records actual outcomes when available.
