## Context and decision

The schema previously typed locks and rates without an inherited operator
contract. Legacy generation reconstructs cells, varies anchors and assigns rate
attributes. That history is preserved, not retrospectively called a violation.
X2 now retains faithful authoring inputs; X6 asks whether a newly defined arm can
make those permissions operative and retain acceptable measured behavior.

The reviewable, pre-RED authority is
`docs/development/cycle-38-operative-mutation-permissions.md`. It defines
`operative_permissions_v1`, exact legacy/off parity, inherited input probabilities,
weighted selection, hard generation locks, separate symmetry permission and
validator-gated actual edits. The algorithm is a deep-copy operator, not a
matrix reconstruction. Actual lifecycle damage remains distinct from genetic
operator permission; runtime repair must not erase the inherited policy before
any future consumer qualification.

The staged G0-G3 gate and conditional full evolution/product gates are fixed in
that contract. An opt-in capability needs safety, variation and quality evidence;
it does not imply generic fitness improvement or permission to flip defaults.
Any failed gate means REFUSE consumer promotion, with the experimental seam and
failing evidence retained. No historical observation is regenerated onto the fix.
