## WHY

Cycle 20 (Phase J) opened the generative range: the breeding surface
learned to spell the ratified vocabulary (motion-trio emission 0.0 →
0.512), repair learned to preserve it, the evolution loop learned to
claim niches faster, and the workshop learned to author constraints
over it. But an opening is not a settlement. Phase J's own gate report
named five things its opening left unsettled, and Phase K is the cycle
that settled each one **by a record with its bars written down first**:

1. Repair PRESERVED the vocabulary but still PERCEIVED it through a
   recorded palette-family coarsening (DR 0039's named future record).
2. The 16×16 generation-layer bake-off verdict had FLIPPED under
   DR 0031's own revisit rule — and had not been decided.
3. Nine descriptor bins stood budget-bounded after the probe's leap,
   their emptiness described as "structured" but never measured.
4. Two real channels were LIT but UNCONSUMED (I3's callback weather
   path, J4's brotli-free density instrument), and `lineage_churn` had
   been silent on every real path without ever being dispositioned.
5. The covenant's earned budget had no authoring face (H4's named
   cross-surface decision).

## THE LADDER

1. **K1 — the 11-class regrowth head** (`aa00b94`, **DR 0041 —
   adoption WITHHELD by its own pre-registered rule**). The head's
   class vocabulary became a model-level property at both head sites
   (the CA lab authority and the promoted `mecha_engine.repair`,
   mirrored verbatim), `legacy` byte-identical by the rider pattern
   and `rich` a 12-way softmax over the full ratified live alphabet
   with identity perception; every consumer derives its alphabet FROM
   THE MODEL. **G1 (pooled exact-token gain ≥ +0.10) REFUSED at
   +0.0944** while G2–G5 all passed — and the refusal carries its own
   measured mechanism: at the 480 rich-token sites the legacy head
   restores **0.0 exactly** (it cannot spell seven of eleven tokens —
   measured, never assumed) against the rich head's **0.525**; the
   2,200 legacy-token sites are statistically identical (0.9032 vs
   0.9036 — the full alphabet costs the shape prior nothing); guard
   substitutions collapse **131 → 5**. The pooled gain is pure
   denominator arithmetic (0.525 × 480/2680 ≈ 0.094). The re-opener is
   NAMED, not smuggled.

2. **K2 — the DR 0031 generation-layer revisit** (`8313628`,
   **DR 0042 ADOPTED on both axes**). The revisit was judged AT THE
   CONSUMER — both generation arms through the FULL adopted loop at
   the matched 20,480-evaluation budget, with the rule pre-registered
   and incumbent-favoring. Cycle 20's own lesson (one-shot verdicts can
   dissolve in the loop) ran in **reverse**: the +0.01 one-shot novelty
   margin became **coverage 0.7852 → 0.8984 (+0.1133, 5.7× the +0.02

   462. The number is structural — **460 occupied niches is exactly the
   support ceiling the occupancy instrument measured**: the loop now
   fills everything its candidate stream is known to reach. The E4
   gates re-ran with the GA baseline carried symmetrically (coverage

   **the DR 0032 GA-advantage clause refused DEEPER: +11.28% vs the
   ≥ +25% bar** — the refutation monotone across the generation layer's

## WHAT PHASE K ACTUALLY SETTLED

The cycle's shape is worth naming, because it is the point. **Three of
the five slices ended in a verb that was not "ship it."** K1 refused
its own adoption at +0.0944 against a +0.10 bar it had written down
first. K3 delivered four bounds rather than four proofs, and made the
bounds worth more by measuring their gaps. K4 dispositioned a channel
as blocked-upstream and pinned the silence instead of inventing a
proxy. Only K2 and K5 adopted — and K2's adoption arrived with a
refutation attached (the DR 0032 clause refused *deeper*, not nearer)
and an honest failure rate recorded (~22% of constrained generation
attempts refused loudly).

That is the settlement: after Phase K the vocabulary is not merely
spellable, it is **accounted for at every layer of its own stack** —
generation adopts it in the loop, repair's perception of it is measured
and its retrain honestly withheld, the descriptor geometry it reaches
is proven to 508/512 with the residue explained, the weather it lives
under is fed by measured biology, and the covenant that constrains it
shows its earned budget to the person authoring it.
