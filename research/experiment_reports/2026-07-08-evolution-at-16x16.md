# Experiment Report: Evolution and the Game Surfaces at 16×16 — A4 Closes (Cycle 09 · ENGINE)

Date: 2026-07-08

## WHY

A1–A3 proved the pipeline at the ratified scale; A4's charter is the
surfaces a player meets: `evolve`, the archive's descriptor bins, the
Keeper session, replays, and the companion — at 16×16 defaults, with
the session's engine parameters stepped up from the toy config (3
generations × 4 population) to what measured wall-clock allows.

## WHAT

1. **Descriptor bins re-derived** (prior commit `84cd2be`): body-mass
   edges scale by the reference canvas (`edge·w·h//64` — identity at
   8×8, [24, 36, 48, 64] at 16×16) at all three binning sites; the mass
   axis spreads ≥4 of 5 bins over the corpus instead of collapsing.
2. **Wall-clock measured, config raised**: one era at 16×16 over the
   50-creature corpus (the session's exact path — trainers, weather,
   learned-legibility parenting):

   | Config | evolve wall-clock | filled niches | qd |
   | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
   | gen 3 × pop 4 (toy) | 0.1 s | 8 | 7.4 |
   | **gen 6 × pop 16 (adopted)** | **1.2 s** | **39** | **36.4** |
   | gen 8 × pop 24 | 3.4 s | 62 | 57.9 |
   | gen 10 × pop 32 | 4.9 s | 70 | 65.4 |

   One-time session training ≈ 6 s. `DEFAULT_ENGINE_PARAMETERS` and
   `run_campaign` defaults are now **6 × 16** — instant-feeling eras,
   with the covenant's earned doublings (up to 48 × 128 at tier 3)
   staying in measured playable territory. The bridge toward Phase E's
   documented scale, not its endpoint.
3. **Unlock thresholds re-tuned to measured magnitudes** (DR 0021:
   tuning data, falsifiable by tests). A measured 3-era campaign at the
   new default: niches 38/33/20, qd 35.4/30.9/18.4 across
   calm/stable/storm. Tiers: niche_mastery [16, 26, 36], quality_
   diversity [9.0, 18.0, 33.0] — the same top-tier-near-measured-max
   design as the 8×8 calibration ([4, 8, 13] against max 13).
   Bloodline/storm/legibility tiers unchanged (their regimes measured
   unchanged). The regenerated demo earns 8 unlock facts over 3 eras —
   a progression, neither instantly maxed nor empty.
4. **Size-coherent eras, scale-aware continuity**: `evolve` rejects a
   founder whose canvas differs from the era's seed corpus THROUGH the
   founder gate (recorded verbatim, like every rejection) instead of
   crashing generation against a mismatched model — the workshop test
   releases both a 16×16 creature (admitted, leads the era) and an 8×8
   one (rejected with the honest reason). `campaign_scale` /
   `session_scale` read a bloodline's scale from its own recorded
   bodies: pre-A4 8×8 campaigns keep their 8×8 machinery; fresh
   campaigns start at 16×16.
5. **The workshop serves both scales**: per-scale generation/repair
   models (8×8 eager as always, 16×16 lazy), preview/live/replay/
   offspring resolve models by the creature's own scale, the creature
   listing leads with the 16×16 corpus, SLIP compat reports both
   corpora, and the companion adopts 16×16 creatures through the same
   engine gate (adoption was already size-generic).
6. **Demo artifacts regenerated** at the new defaults: the stage-0
   session report, campaign ledger, and all three era replays.

## FINDINGS

- Wall-clock is NOT the binding constraint at this scale — 16×16
  evolution is effectively free up to populations ~32 in stdlib. The
  binding constraint on Phase E scale will be evaluations (20k+), not
  per-era cost.
- Per-era `max_lineage_generation` stays 2 at any config (lineage
  deepens across ERAS through founder chains, not within an era) —
  bloodline tiers correctly stayed untouched.
- Deeper configs monotonically raise niches/qd (8 → 70 filled niches
  from toy to gen10×pop32): the archive axes, re-binned at scale, have
  the headroom Phase E needs.
