# Experiment Report: animstate at Documented Scale — 950 Clip Frames (Cycle 11 · ENGINE · C3)

Date: 2026-07-08

## WHY

The documents name `animstate_300` — at least 300 labeled animation
samples — and the og dataset delivered exactly 300 single-frame state
projections at 8×8. With the clip system in place (C1/C2), the honest
resolution is the clip FRAME, and the documented data product needed
regenerating at the ratified scale for its named consumer: Phase D's
animation-preference learner (the documented bandit over clips).

## WHAT

`labs/animation_system/animstate_dataset.py` (RED-first; the animation
suite is now 21 tests): every (creature × clip × frame) over the 16×16
corpus and the full clip vocabulary — **950 labeled samples**, each
carrying grid_id, palette, clip, the frame's state-track entry,
frame_index/frame_count, packet-shaped cells, and the anchor. Naming
decision recorded: the dataset is named by its real size
(`animstate_950`) and carries a `supersedes` block naming the
documented `animstate_300` and its minimum — the og 8×8 dataset stays
in `data/grid_genome/` as history. A data-product README ships with it.

## FINDINGS

| Gate | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Sample count (documented minimum 300) | **950** |
| Coverage: creatures × clips × frames | complete (50 × 5 × per-clip frames) |
| Frames in bounds | 1.0 |
| Frames palette-clean | 1.0 |
| All five palettes and all five states labeled | yes |
| Deterministic | yes |

Samples per clip: idle/beg/ill/sleep 200 each, blink 150 (three-frame
clip).
