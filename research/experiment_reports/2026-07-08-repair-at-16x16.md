# Experiment Report: Repair Re-proven at 16×16 — and the NumPy Trigger That Didn't Fire (Cycle 09 · ENGINE · A3)

Date: 2026-07-08

## WHY

The repair feature layout was structurally 8×8: a 64-slot position
one-hot with the bias at index 160, `range(8)` scans in training and the
blind arm, all mirrored bit-for-bit between the lab origin
(`ca_nca_growth_repair`) and the promoted `mecha_engine.repair`. At
16×16 the old layout silently repaired only the top-left quadrant — or
crashed on position indices past the weight width. A3 requires repair
retrained and re-gated at scale, wound-site semantics unchanged, 8×8
weights bit-for-bit. The TODO also names repair training as the likely
spot where "NumPy enters if the scale demands it" — a claim that needed
a measurement, not an assumption.

## WHAT

Matched-pair surgery, lab and package identical (RED-first via
`labs/cellular_automata/test_repair_scale16.py` — 40 errors before):

1. **Size-parametric feature layout**: `neighborhood_features` derives
   dimensions from the matrix (bounds, position index `y·w + x`, bias at
   `base + w·h`); `feature_count_for(w, h)` generalizes the count
   (161 at 8×8 — the historical constant exactly — 353 at 16×16);
   `train_softmax` takes the count as an additive keyword defaulting to
   the historical value.
2. **Training derives its scale from the corpus** (single-size check;
   mixed sizes fail loudly — they would silently corrupt the position
   one-hot); the model records `width`/`height`.
3. **Scale guard at application**: a model asked to repair a grid of a
   different scale raises `repair model was trained at AxA but the grid
   is BxB` (pre-A3 models without scale keys are treated as 8×8). Wound
   fill order, guard substitutions, metadata, and grid-id suffix are
   untouched — wound-site semantics unchanged.

## FINDINGS

- **8×8 is bit-for-bit intact**: the full parity suite (40-grid
  training, model dicts compared field-for-field, wound repair guard
  on/off over held-out grids) passed unmodified, as did every repair
  consumer (package 291, runtime 65, game_loop 81, repair_boundary 4).
- **The 16×16 stability re-gate** (10-grid training slice, dropout 0.2,
  the benchmark seeds, all 40 held-out corpus creatures): guarded
  wound repair returns a validator-clean creature **40/40**, refills
  every reported wound site including those beyond the old 8×8
  quadrant, and restores the exact destroyed token at **539/603 wound
  sites (0.894)** — approaching the historic 8×8 assisted bar (0.9,
  measured there on full-canvas overlap with a 40-grid model, so the
  numbers are near neighbors, not identical metrics).
- **The NumPy trigger did NOT fire** (negative result, recorded):
  full-corpus 16×16 training with production hyperparameters measured
  **21.0 s** stdlib (40,198 examples, 353 features) vs 5.5 s at 8×8
  (10,809 examples, 161 features) — a ~4× cost for a train-once step,
  nowhere near demanding a dependency at this phase. Test suites train
  on a 10-grid slice (~2.4 s) to keep the sweep fast. NumPy waits for a
  scale that actually demands it (Phase E's population regime is the
  named candidate).
