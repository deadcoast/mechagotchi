# Experiment Report: The 16×16 Seed Corpus and the Size-Parametric Canvas (Cycle 09 · ENGINE · A2)

Date: 2026-07-08

## WHY

A1 ratified 16×16 as the proven default scale (DR 0029) with ten pilot
exemplars as measurement subjects. The documents' corpus deliverable at
the primary scale — ≥50 hand-authored-quality creatures with real
morphological variety, plus the invalid counterexample corpus — did not
exist, and the workshop's canvas (the grid is the hero surface) was
pinned to 8×8. Without the corpus, A3 has nothing to train or re-gate
on.

## WHAT

1. **The rich-token authoring path became shared infrastructure**:
   `grid_helpers.grid_from_rich_matrix` (+ `RICH_TOKENS`,
   `RICH_ORGAN_ROLES`) is now the single source for the DR 0015
   alphabet (finding L-04 discipline); `invariant_scaling`'s pilot
   builder delegates to it, behavior pinned unchanged by its 22 tests.
2. **The corpus**: `corpus_builder.BLUEPRINTS_16` + `build_seed_grids_16`
   — seeds 001–010 are the pilots under corpus identity; 011–050 are
   forty newly authored creatures, eight per palette: mantis, snail,
   sprout, hopper, drone, lantern, pupfish, warden (lcd_green); bat,
   scorpion, hermit, rat, horned beetle, automaton, spider, mole
   (gameboy_gray); scarab, cobra, ant, fennec, sandworm, cat, ember,
   obelisk (amber_terminal); squid, anglerfish, crane, nautilus,
   frostbot, ray, seahorse, beacon (crt_blue); bunny, luna moth, imp,
   fungus, ghost, weaver, koi, shrine (pocket_plum). Profile-view
   asymmetric creatures (snail, rat, mole, cat, anglerfish, crane,
   seahorse, koi, hermit, sandworm, pupfish) sit alongside bilateral
   ones; limbless forms (obelisk, ghost, ember) alongside limbed;
   lean (strider 18, ember 24) alongside heavy (fennec 76, ray 80;
   corpus max 85, median 51.5).
3. **The invalid corpus**: the same four structural invalidators as the
   8×8 corpus applied per seed → 200 counterexamples, all rejected.
4. **The size-parametric canvas**: the grid editor gained 8×8/16×16
   size buttons (resizing preserves the drawing's overlap; loads and
   imports adopt the grid's own scale), and the workshop server's
   matrix gate accepts exactly the two ratified sizes; the morphology
   panel's symmetry now mirrors on the grid's own width.

## FINDINGS

| Corpus gate (pinned by `Corpus16Tests`) | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 50 seeds, unique ids, validator-clean at [16,16] | pass |
| Core floor ≥18 on every seed (DR 0029) | pass (span 18–85, median 51.5) |
| Sensor pair on every seed (the A2 hard corpus gate) | pass |
| Exactly one animation anchor per seed | pass |
| Ten creatures per palette, all five palettes | pass |
| Full DR 0015 cell-role vocabulary exercised | pass |
| 50 distinct silhouettes; ≥40 seeds beyond body/eye/mouth | pass (50/50 rich) |
| 200 invalid counterexamples, all rejected | pass |

- The editor's basic token alphabet (B/A/E/M + trait overrides) carries
  to 16×16 unchanged; the matrix↔grid converters and the PNG/APNG/
  Aseprite/spritesheet codecs were already size-generic (they read
  `grid_size`), so no codec changed.
- **Honest limitations** (engine truth, not workshop defects): PNG/APNG
  export of a 16×16 creature is still blocked by SLIP alignment because
  every shipped profile's `body_mass` bounds are 8×8-denominated —
  size-relative SLIP bounds are A3 (DR 0029 decision 7). The morphology
  panel's `stress_load` mirrors the engine's own 8×8-calibrated
  `mass/16` probe verbatim, so 16×16 bodies read saturated until the
  A3/A4 renormalization; the mirror follows the engine, never leads it.
  The engine validator's core floor is still the 8×8 constant 4 at all
  sizes (A3), so the corpus's 18-floor is enforced by the corpus gate,
  not yet by the engine.

## VERDICT

The documented corpus deliverable at the primary scale exists and is
gated: 50 hand-authored 16×16 creatures with measured variety, 200
counterexamples, and an authoring canvas at both ratified scales. A3
(generator/validator/repair/lifecycle re-proven at 16×16) now has its
training and gating material.
