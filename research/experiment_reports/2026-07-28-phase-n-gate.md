## THE CYCLE'S CHARACTER, RECORDED AS THE FINDING

Phase L audited its own **numbers**. Phase M audited its own
**sentences**. **Phase N audited its own INSTRUMENTS** — and the
instrument was wrong more often than the engine was.

**Every one of the six slices found a defect in the measuring apparatus
rather than in the thing measured:**

1. **N1** — DR 0046's two controls were **single draws** and
   understated. The four-pair means are 0.316917 and 0.371583 against
   the recorded 0.200000 and 0.310333; M2 had drawn the most favourable
   incumbent pair on the ladder.
2. **N2** — the queue's description of the defect was **wrong**.
   `reachability_probe` *does* record its edges; it is
   `occupancy_saturation` and `reachability_escalation` that record
   none, which is worse.
3. **N3** — **my own observer** scored the Bayesian counter against an
   event the thread cannot observe, producing −154.796%. Corrected, it
   reads **+2.756%**.
4. **N4** — **my own population parameter** was invented rather than
   inherited (512 against DR 0047's 256), and **it flipped the
   verdict**: `insertion` failed a gate by 0.000652.
5. **N7** — the obvious migration plan would have **destroyed
   evidence**: regenerating `generation_refusal_causes` under the fix
   reports zero refusals, erasing the finding it exists to carry.
6. **N5** — **my own candidate's name overclaimed** (`lethal_margin`
   over thresholds one of which is not lethal), in a study whose subject
   is whether a label overstates what it measures.

**Three of those six are errors this session made and caught before they
shipped as findings.** They are recorded as process deviations in their
own reports, not absorbed.

## The measured results

### N1 — the derivation protocol (DR 0049)

Re-addressing as a function of derivation size, 75 → 4,800, four
disjoint seed pairs per rung, both recipes.

- **Gate 1 `sampling_limited`**: slope **−0.4186** / **−0.3997** against
  sampling theory's −0.5, judged by the measured spread of four
  independent per-track slopes. **Structural indeterminacy refuted** —
  R(N) falls monotonically 0.7268 → **0.1278**.
- **Gate 2 splits from Gate 1, and the split is the finding**: at
  N=600 the incumbent re-addresses **31.7% of individual creatures**
  while its occupied-niche count moves **exactly the measured floor**.
  At N=4,800 re-addressing improves 2.5× while ceiling movement is
  *higher*.
- **`derivation_count` stays at 600** — what raising it would fix is not
  what was broken.
- **Measure-thinness is structural**: the `visual_complexity` band is
  constant across a 64× range (0.043993 → 0.045196). Only the edge
  *placement* inside it is sampled.
- The instrument reproduced DR 0046's committed controls exactly.

### N2 — the probe geometry frozen (DR 0050)

All four gates passed. **G3 recomputed both committed headlines from
scratch and both came back exact** — support ceiling **460 → 460**,
reached-by-generators **495 → 495**. G4 separation held at
**0.037500**, precisely L3's split, so DR 0046's refusal to converge is
intact. The recipe now lives in one place instead of three private
copies, every probe-side artifact records `edges_source`, and a parity
pin makes drift fail the suite.

**Honest limit stated with the win**: freezing the edges does **not**
freeze the numbers — the support ceiling ranges **447–462** across six
evaluation resamples.

### N4 / N7 — the `slip_admission` default (DR 0052, DR 0053)

**Decided: `insertion`.** `parents` fails archive legality completely
(35/37/35 illegal elites, evicting none); `bootstrap` and `insertion`
both clear all four gates and are indistinguishable on the gated
dimensions, so DR 0047's own invasiveness tie-break decides. **Cost
stated, not softened**: 0.029948 coverage, **90% of the measured
control**.

**Then flipped (N7), and the migration was not a regeneration.** The
inventory found **eight** implicit callers, not the six DR 0052
estimated. Regenerating them would have destroyed evidence, so the
correct migration was to flip the default and make every historical
study **name its arm**. **DR 0053's rule**: *any setting that changes
what an artifact means is recorded at the call site that produced it,
never inherited from a default; and a study that measures a defect is
never migrated onto the fix.*

**Prospective by design** — no committed number moves, and every new
consumer gets the fix by default, pinned.

### N5 — `health` (DR 0054)

**`grid_stability` under that name: REFUSED as a euphemism.** The
neglected creature **starved to death** and finished with
`grid_stability` at **0.927** — a bar reading it shows **93% on a
corpse** — and feeding a starving companion moves it **−0.001**, *down*.
An inverted signal, not a weak one.

**`health` ADOPTED as `danger_margin`**, the minimum normalised safety
margin across the engine's own thresholds, which answers DR 0048's
objection that a composite needs undocumented weights: a minimum has
none. Reads 0.0 on the dead creature, 0.845 on the survivor, **+0.176**
when the keeper feeds a starving companion.

**The game already got this right** — it displays the axis as *"body
stability"* and already shows `stress` too, so §1.17's visible/hidden
split is **stale against the shipped client**. What shipped is the
**guard**: five pins making the refusal an enforced property.

## Limitations of this cycle's own pre-registrations, recorded not repaired

- **N1's Gate 2 assumed monotone convergence** and the movement is not
  monotone; the incumbent passes by an **exact tie** against a floor
  measured from three pairs. `determined_at = 600` is a noisy statistic
  crossing a threshold, not a located stability point.
- **N5's G3 rests on n=2**, not the six runs the artifact lists — the
  arms are deterministic over one seed grid.
- **N3's E baseline is uniform random** (the documented precedent's
  shape, but a weak opponent), and **F's stump baseline gets a
  full-sample oracle** while the tree is scored online.
- **N4/N7 measure one SLIP profile**, DR 0047's own inherited limit.
