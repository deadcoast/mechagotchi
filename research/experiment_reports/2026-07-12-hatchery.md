# Experiment Report: The Hatchery — Species Generation in the Client (Cycle 15 · GAME · F8)

Date: 2026-07-12

## WHY

MVP 0.2's generation rung (validity §1.23): generation must stop
being a fixtures-only path — the founding vision's creatures are
"born from procedural genomes," and until now the client's creatures
came from the committed corpus or from death-succession only. The
promoted `generation` module has carried the full capability since
cycle 02; F8 surfaces it to the player.

## WHAT

**`game/src/mecha_game/hatchery.py`** (RED-first, 4 tests): a thin,
honest veneer over the engine — `hatch_founders` calls
`generation.generate_batch` under the ACTIVE covenant
(profile-weighted parent filtering, validator-gated output,
deterministic unique ids — all the engine's own guarantees) and pairs
every candidate with `explain_generation`'s VERBATIM trace;
`origin_summary` builds the display line from trace fields only —
the creature's provenance is engine truth, never a caption.

**The campaign gained the lab's E5 seam**: `advance_era(...,
released_grids=…)` — released creatures LEAD the founder pool ahead
of the prior era's elites (the T1/DR 0016 founders-lead rule), with
admission or verbatim rejection judged by `evolve` itself.

**The client**: **H** hatches a founder under the active covenant and
adopts it (the footer shows its origin line — strategy, parent,
proposals accepted/attempted, generation); **R** releases the current
companion's genome as a founder for the next era. The creator loop
closes: hatch → raise → release → evolve → adopt the elite.

**Measured** (`data/game_client/hatchery_stage0.json`, status
complete, session scale): three founders hatched deterministically
(twin runs byte-equal) — e.g. `constrained_wfc_style_stabilizer from
mg_seed16_017_pupfish · proposals 11/12 · gen 1` — all three
covenant-aligned and adoptable through the engine's own gate; the
released founder led the next era's pool and the era survived.

## HONEST REMAINDER

1. **The hatchery is the constrained stabilizer, not WFC** — only
   constrained candidates carry the explanation trace; DR 0031's WFC
   remains the era-side production generator inside the engine. A WFC
   hatchery path would need an engine-side trace seam — named, not
   smuggled.
2. **Workshop releases are F9** — this rung releases hatched/adopted
   genomes; the authored-creature bridge is next.
3. **Hatch seeds are per-launch counters** (player actions are the
   loop's nondeterminism, as ever); the evidence harness pins the
   deterministic path under fixed seeds.
