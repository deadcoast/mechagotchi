## THE CYCLE'S CHARACTER, RECORDED AS THE FINDING

**The visible-axis program is COMPLETE.** §1.17 named eight visible
companion axes in the founding vision; when this cycle opened, two had
no engine source at all. At the gate: **seven on the player's screen,
one an honest opt-in, zero without an engine source** — and every step
of that closure is a gated record (two CHOSEN definitions that say
`chosen` on the surface; one opt-in seam proven byte-identical off).

**And the instruments moved with the surfaces, deliberately.** The P1
split instrument was built to raise on any unexplained difference from
the committed M5 survey; by cycle's end it expects exactly **three**
DR-explained differences (health/0054, happiness/0058,
cleanliness/0059) and still raises on a fourth. Every slice ran the
reproduce-a-committed-number-first hard gate (M5's dispositions, N5's
0.0685 floor, the Stage 0 episodes, N3's full statistic set, the two
probe bodies) — five instruments verified against committed evidence
before a single new number was trusted.

**The predictions did their job in both directions.** P1's and P3's
held (including P3's fairness finding: an attentive keeper's care
absorbs the dirt pressure entirely). P2's held *including its
deliberately named uncertainty* (the engine's own `need_balance`
statistic fell under the floor exactly where the prediction said it
might). **P4's was REFUTED, and the refutation is the cycle's sharpest
finding**: the incumbent frozen reactive rule — welfare-optimal at the
survival level since D6 — turned out to leave per-tick regret on the
table barely better than uniform random, and the Q-table beats **the
incumbent itself** by 44.685% on the incumbent's own replayed states.

**Three process deviations were caught and recorded, none absorbed**:
P3's first probe idled a companion to death and tried to clean the
corpse (the engine's refusal became its own pin); P3's first
clean-stream starved its subject; P5's first comparison enumeration
under-specified DR 0050's provenance keys and briefly verdicted a
zero-difference restatement as "with deltas."

### P2 — `happiness` (DR 0058)

Three CHOSEN candidates reading only what the engine records; five
pre-registered gates — N5's four plus **G5 distinct-from-shown**, whose
`refuse_as_duplicate` outranks the euphemism verdict. `bond_rename`
passed every behavioural gate and was refused on exactly one ground:
its per-tick series IS the shown bond readout's. `need_balance` — the
engine's own statistic — was withheld under the 0.0685 floor with its
corpse reading (0.65) recorded. **`social_warmth` adopted with the
neglected corpse reading 0.0**, and the readout landed the same day
with `chosen: true` and its legibility in the window's own counts.
**Live in the client: `play` raises happiness while it lowers health**
(+0.083 vs −0.075) — two bars, different questions, both true.
Game 216 → 228.

### P3 — `cleanliness` (DR 0059)

The engine's first OPT-IN lifecycle mechanic — the one §1.17 gap no
display work could close. Every parameter DERIVED (median incumbent
drift; mutate's own grid cost; feed's own hunger relief; the engine's
own need→stress coefficient); byte-identical off, proven against the
committed Stage 0 comparator's own numbers and a frozen key list;
non-lethal; the companion cannot clean itself; you cannot clean a
corpse. Five gates with the charter's refusals first-class: **ADOPT**
— the neglect stream pays an exact deterministic price (+0.00682
stress, the tick-11 starvation unmoved), the switchboard's welfare
moves 1.6e-05 against the 5% bar, and **an attentive keeper's care
absorbs the dirt pressure entirely**. Landed for opted-in covenants;
the default flip is a NAMED record with its regeneration cascade
priced. Package 299 → 316; game 228 → 239; lifecycle_sim 24 → 43.

### P4 — the harder baselines (dated notes beside DR 0051)

## Limitations of this cycle's own work, recorded not repaired

- **P2's definition is one-input**: a tended-while-dying companion
  reads happy beside a collapsing health bar — legible by design, and
  a different documented definition is a different gate. The 5-tick
  window is the engine's bound, not tuned.
- **P3's reach is opt-in**: no default player has the mechanic until
  the SLIP rung or the flip record lands; there is no dirt beg-display,
  and dirt is invisible to `need_balance` by design.
- **P4's regret is empirical**, computed against the harness's own
  behaviour distribution, not a counterfactual simulator; the online
  stump is one pre-registered operationalization.
- **P5 proves provenance, not stability**: the support ceiling still
  ranges 447–462 across resamples.
- **P1's hidden half is still unsurveyed**: the six unsurfaced hidden
  axes' engine sources remain M5's remainder 4.
