## THE CYCLE'S CHARACTER, RECORDED AS THE FINDING

Phase N adopted three things and put none of them in front of a player
— correct each time, deliberate each time, and exactly the debt that
quietly becomes permanent. **Cycle 25 paid it without letting a single
committed number drift:**

- **O1/O2 landed N5's adopted definition** on the package surface and
  the player's screen — and the landing surfaced a real design question
  (the play-drop) whose two easy answers were forbidden by DR 0021's
  first law. The shipped answer costs no new number.
- **O3 moved the headline onto the adopted default by RESTATING, never
  overwriting** — only the arm that can move was re-run (ten runs, not
  forty), both incumbent artifacts stand untouched, and the delta is
  priced in both directions (nine niches and 0.0176 coverage buy
  +7.12 QD, zero refusals, zero illegal elites).
- **O4 closed the generation track's oldest disposition debt by first
  correcting its own charter** — the bake-off-both framing would have
  tested shape grammar at a job the dossier never gave it. Both
  verdicts came from pre-registered rules whose bars are committed
  evidence (the B2 validity bar, the refused arm's distinct floor, a
  two-seed measured noise floor), and the prediction recorded before
  the run was confirmed on both arms.

**Every landing was additive**: no incumbent moved, no committed
artifact was regenerated, and the one mid-cycle process failure — a RED
