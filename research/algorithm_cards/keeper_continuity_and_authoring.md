# Keeper continuity and faithful authoring

Inputs are saved companion facts, validated campaign state/SQLite ledger, pending
completion journal and complete authoring documents. Outputs are factual life
records, coherent recovery decisions, lossless modern exchange and revision-bound
browser feedback. This is persistence and interaction work; no new scientific
algorithm or adoption threshold is proposed.

New actual life IDs use a Keeper-prefixed independent token. Existing lineage
records keep known facts, missing legacy observations are marked unavailable, and
the old succession index separately determines child seeds. Bounded recent care
events accompany observed counters. Validation rejects contradictory identities,
outcomes and counters rather than inventing repairs.

Campaign validation checks identity, metadata, exact era prefix, seeds, unlock facts,
status and valid elite survivors. Completion accepts only its original prefix or
the exact already-saved result. SQLite backup and immutable digest-addressed state
snapshots publish a manifest last and retain latest/prior. Explicit restore preserves
primary bytes and uses a marker until the pair validates; interrupted work is blocked
and retryable. Cost observations are tiny-fixture measurements, not scale guarantees.

Modern Workshop requests carry a complete mecha_workshop_document_v1, draft revision,
canonical digest and expected disk revision. Serialized writes retain unique backups
and roll back immediate exceptions. Browser undo/redo/recovery preserve unknown valid
fields and layers, reject stale responses and keep failed drafts available. Four
modern carriers bind selected semantic documents; legacy arms are byte-pinned.

Limits: browser storage can fail and offers download; in-process serialization is
not a cross-process transaction. Historic missing care cannot be reconstructed.
Native keyboard and human usability remain unobserved. Report, DR0081 and the Cycle38
contract provide actual commands, control parity, demonstrations and closing gates.
