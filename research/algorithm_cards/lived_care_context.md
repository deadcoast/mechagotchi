# Lived care context

## Name

observed_care_selection_v1; keeper_care_context_v1; current native thought.

## Purpose

Preserve actual care associated with immutable founders, test a bounded contextual
succession selector, and show truthful native deliberation.

## Mathematical basis

For each of eight fixed-seed candidates evaluate a single observed keeper window of
length H. Welfare = sum(existing per-tick balance)/H, with dead remainder zero.
Choose lexicographic maximum (welfare, survival ticks, negative candidate index).
The named archive morphology probe reuses its calibrated arithmetic on evaluation
copies; pristine evaluation is an independent null control. Archive fitness keeps
its existing common-horizon evaluation.

## Inputs

Frozen parent genome, generation model, effective covenant, base seed, exact bounded
care events/summary/observation clocks, explicit policy and evaluator. No learned Q.

## Outputs

Generated child, full candidate/selection receipt, immutable care provenance and
current-life/current-tick thought text from recorded deliberation depth/action.

## Strengths

Falsifiable null/effect controls; immutable occurrence association; voluntary feeding;
no history rewriting or implicit threshold change; bounded resumable evidence.

## Weaknesses

Pristine candidates can share physiology; contextual selection may produce no
structural effect. A bounded late-life tape is a standardized test input rather than
a faithful life replay. A morphology stress probe does not establish live personality.

## Failure modes

Mixed identities, fabricated tail actions, report-only provenance effects, metadata
ranking, stale thought, inherited Q, incomparable horizons or incomplete promotion.

## Integration risk

Duplicate IDs, archive replacement and restart must preserve actual occurrence links.
Malformed or contradictory evidence refuses before save mutation. Disabled and absent
paths keep incumbent genetic/scientific results; factual metadata is additive.

## License status

Repository Apache-2.0 implementation; no new external dependency or asset.

## Prototype result

Both care evaluators completed all 30 pairs with zero structural differences: REFUSE. Care affected scores but never changed rankings. Elapsed survival ticks also conceal terminal death; a versioned terminal-survival correction and distinct holdouts remain open. The separate product learning cohort completed all 1,500 lives and passed both 5% gates: scientific ADOPT, with native default unchanged. See the dated report and retained runner state.

## Decision

Experimental, default off. No scientific or personality adoption implied.

## Go-No-Go gate

Complete registered care cohort, structural paired effect, index0 nonregression,
metadata/null controls, exact off parity, faithful persistence/reproduction and
current native thought checks. R3 learning requires >=5% against BOTH comparators
plus unchanged care controls. A missing mandatory result remains REFUSE.
