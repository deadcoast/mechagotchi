# Shared body workload response

## Name
Opt-in workload-v1 play energy response.

## Purpose
Connect semantic inherited body capacity to a real action in native play and evaluation while keeping species behavior priors and existing care/ecology contracts intact.

## Mathematical basis
For M occupied cells and F intact body_core/locomotion capacity, play energy cost is round(0.07*M/max(F,1,M*0.07/0.34),6). Integrity is one minus cell damage; unique semantic coordinates are summed deterministically. Reference cost and cap derive from existing play cost and sleep restoration. This is proposed game design, not calibrated biology.

## Inputs
Validated actual initial/repaired grid, explicit legacy/workload-v1 model. No history hash, personality descriptor, learned Q-table or covenant bias enters the projection.

## Outputs
Active-only model/projection state, conditional action history and measured native/evaluator consequences. Legacy output remains unchanged.

## Strengths
One legible action interaction, deterministic shared arithmetic and explicit model provenance.

## Weaknesses
A monotone workload response may preserve care rankings; fixed-body episodes still reset and full repair remains. No food scarcity, continuous age or finite reproduction is modeled.

## Failure modes
Malformed integrity/projection, model conflicts, stale input closure, ordinal score changes without changed selected structure, or engineered fixture evidence misrepresented as production outcomes.

## Integration risk
Identity must persist through current lives, cold newborns, care contexts, immutable eras, restart, recovery and reproduction.

## License status
Repository implementation under its existing Apache-2.0 terms; no new external asset/dependency.

## Prototype result
Implemented and measured: pristine care qualifies narrowly at 9/30 jointly changed selected structures/rankings; morphology changes 4/30 structures but fails one welfare nonregression, so aggregate care REFUSES. All three production pairs change actual trajectories/actions and descendant/archive structures; none becomes extinct. Exact reproduction and detached Windows delivery pass. Original refusals remain.

## Decision
Prototype only, opt-in; no default promotion.

## Go-No-Go gate
Registered causal play/no-play and consumed repaired-body contrast/null, retained off baselines and operational regression gates precede untouched 30-pair care holdouts. Each evaluator retains v2 nonregression and structural-descendant criteria. Three paired production seeds observe survival without promising extinction. No tuning or additional seeds.
