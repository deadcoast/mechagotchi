## Name / Purpose

`operative_permissions_v1`: deterministic offspring operations controlled by
faithful authored rates, weights and newly specified inherited locks.

## Mathematical basis

Twelve bounded rounds of rate-gated proposals; positive source weights define a
categorical distribution. Structural proposals reducing role-aware horizontal
symmetry require an additional independent Bernoulli permission. Full structural
and SLIP validators accept or atomically reject proposals. Rates are opportunity
probabilities, not observed change frequencies or rewritten output attributes.

## Inputs / Outputs

Validated aligned parent, learned adjacency/token model, optional SLIP, seed,
explicit arm and bounded proposal budget -> full rich child plus detached actual
operation trace and direct ancestry. The parent and all input objects stay intact.

## Strengths

Explicit zero/lock/weight meanings; preserves rich fields and legacy branch;
rejections and no-ops remain inspectable. No forced anchor or rate variation.

## Weaknesses / Failure modes

Permission controls may lower diversity and archive coverage. Validity filtering
changes accepted frequencies. Locks can prevent proposals. Generation locks are
not physical damage immunity; existing repair reconstruction loses policy unless
an additional qualified consumer carries it. Unknown old history is not inferred.

## Integration risk

Only the experimental generation seam is initially implemented. Preview, hatch,
succession, campaign and X5 reproduction require the preregistered qualification
and one explicitly persisted policy; absent configurations remain legacy.

## License status

Original repository implementation, Apache-2.0; no new dependencies.
