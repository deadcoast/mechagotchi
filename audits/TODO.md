# Development TODO — Cycle 24: SHARED track · Phase N — the descriptor space earns its edges

**Planned from the documentation and from cycle 23's own named
remainders** (the standing rule: the developer plans when a queue
exhausts).

Phase M turned the instruments around to face the archive's real
inhabitants, and answered every question it set itself. But three of its
five slices found that **a claim in the repo's own prose was wrong** —
L4's remainder understated, the recurrent-family count stale at 52 when
the artifact had said 54 since `f8c171b`, and the visible-state gap
overstated at five when it is three. Each was found the same way: by
**reading the engine instead of the document**.

And underneath all of it, M2 surfaced the deepest thing this repo has
found in several cycles:

> **The octile edges are not determined by a 600-creature derivation.**
> `visual_complexity` reassigns **26% of the population** when the
> derivation is merely resampled, and its seven interior edges sit
> inside a band about **0.046 wide**. The "two geometries" were never
> two coordinate systems — they were two draws from a noisy process.

That is a defect **underneath every coverage number in the ledger**, and
it is a question about E1's *derivation protocol*, not about which
population feeds it. Cycle 24 is where the descriptor space earns the
edges every other number is quoted against.

**The identity constraint and the anti-periscope rule both apply.**
Cycles 22 and 23 were archive-heavy. This cycle keeps its foundational
work to the two slices that sit *under* everything (N1, N2), pays the
two debts previous records explicitly asked for (N3, N4), and takes one
**GAME-track** slice (N5) plus one deliberately **non-archive** slice
from the still-unmet documentation (N3 is the learning fleet). Community

- [ ] **N1 · Do 600 creatures determine the octile edges?** M2's named
  follow-on, and the deepest defect cycle 23 found. E1 derives the
  scale geometry from a 600-candidate population; M2 measured that
  re-deriving the same recipe at another seed **re-addresses 20–31% of
  a population**, driven almost entirely by `visual_complexity`, whose
  edges sit in a ~0.046-wide band. **Measure edge stability as a
  function of derivation size** — pre-register what "determined" means
  (an edge-movement or re-addressing bar derived from evidence, never a
  constant) and report the size at which the geometry stops moving, or
  report honestly that it does not stabilise within reach. This changes
  no geometry: it measures whether the protocol that produces them is
  sound. If it is not, say so plainly — every coverage number stays
  true under the edges it was measured with (DR 0046's rule), but what
  those edges *mean* would change.

## Standing note

The engine-truth law (DR 0021), the identity constraint, the triad
discipline, the mirror-never-import boundary, Apache-2.0, and DR 0028
apply. Gates are PRE-REGISTERED before the run and a gate that cannot
fail is a bug; new seams are additive and byte-identical when off,
proven not asserted; adoption never deletes the incumbent. **DR 0046's
comparability rule is now law**: numbers measured in different
geometries may not be read against each other, and any claim that does
must be re-measured in one geometry before it is restated.

**Phase M's lesson rides along, and it is about documents rather than
numbers**: three of its five slices found the repo's own prose wrong
where the engine was right. **Read the engine, not the sentence about
the engine** — and when the two disagree, add a dated note beside the
sentence and never rewrite it.
