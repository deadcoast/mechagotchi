# Community Profile Packs

`mechagotchi_pack_v1` files (F16, cycle 16 — the FORMAT rung of the
documented community surface; distribution infrastructure stays
Phase G): SLIPs + creatures + provenance in one importable file.
Built by `mecha_game.packs.build_pack` (which refuses invalid content
with the validators' verbatim errors); imported in the client with
**K** (items re-judged and staged into `keeper/covenants/` and
`keeper/released/`, where the era admission gates judge creatures a
third time at run).

- `mechagotchi_starter_pack.json` - the example pack, built
  deterministically from COMMITTED material: the four valid zoo
  covenants, the two committed workshop releases (8x8-era pieces -
  a 16x16 era rejects them verbatim on size, the documented honest
  outcome), and the corpus beetle. Regenerated by
  `game/evidence.py`.

Produced by `game/evidence.py` (`build_community_pack_report`).
