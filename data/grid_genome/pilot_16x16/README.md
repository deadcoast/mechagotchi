Ten hand-authored 16x16 creatures (cycle 09 A1, DR 0029): the
measurement subjects for the invariant re-derivation at the
documented primary scale. Real morphology from the DR 0015
vocabulary — shell, joint, limb, antenna, sensor, decoration,
effect — across all five palettes, with deliberate probes: a
lean 18-cell-core strider at the documented floor, a 60-cell
turtle near the occupancy ceiling (whose neck makes the core
8- but not 4-connected), a low-symmetry serpent, and a
mid-symmetry golem. NOT upscaled 8x8 blobs. The A2 seed corpus
grows from these.
