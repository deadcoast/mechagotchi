Mechagotchi / readability rating

You are about to look at 21 tiny pixel creatures and rate how readable each one is — whether a person can tell what they're looking at. Each creature gets five ratings on a 1–5 scale (1 = not at all, 5 = clearly yes):

  • silhouetteCan you recognize a creature body from the outline alone?
  • organ_clarityCan you spot eyes/sensors, a mouth, and a main body?
  • palette_contrastDo the colors separate body, features, and background?
  • animation_readinessIs there an obvious feature that could move or blink?
  • companion_appealDoes it read as a plausible little companion, not noise?

Go with your gut — first impressions are exactly what this calibration needs. The full set needs 3 reviewers (63 rows total). Each person does one pass; you can hand this page to someone else for the next pass. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can close and come back.
